Prototypes

Proofs of shape, kept in one place — every prototype ever built in this house, filed under MathSphere Nexus and its project.

A prototype here is a small working build of a design: made to be run and tested, not merely described. It proves a shape — that the design's moving parts fit and behave the way the design says. It is not the product, and this page never calls it one; the product builds stand in the roadmap, in their order.

One rule governs what may be stored here: a prototype must pass house, or it doesn't count as evidence. Passing house means the house laws hold inside it — actions written to a hash-chained receipts record, destructive actions asking twice, the project's own code discipline, and every feature tested live with a document a reader can check. A sketch that fails is a sketch, and it is labeled one or left off.

Every entry states the day its prototype went into implementation and carries its Terminal Record: the dated terminal proof of its tests, parsed in full — how to use everything that was tested and proved. Where no such record exists for a prototype, the entry says so; the absence is shown, never papered over.
