Site Map

Every page on this site, listed

This house publishes its claims so they can be checked. This page is the checking made navigable: every page on the site, in one list, grouped the way the house groups its work. If a page exists here, it is linked here. Every entry also carries a status label, so the maturity of the thing it describes is stated before you click.

How to read the labels

Every entry carries a status label — the maturity of the thing the page describes, stated before you click:

  • Released — software published and available now.
  • In development — software being built; not yet released.
  • Prototype — a small working proof of a design; not the product.
  • Evidence — the record of what was run, and what it showed.
  • Record — a dated entry in the house’s public record.
  • Statement — the house stating what it is, and what it is doing.
  • Governing document — the law the software is built under.
  • Index — a page whose job is to list other pages.
