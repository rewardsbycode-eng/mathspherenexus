IN DEVELOPMENT

Wizard Central Pro AI Modes Toolkit — a gate between a person and their AI models. Every tool call a model makes crosses one checkpoint; the person grants access, reviews what the model proposes, and approves anything that matters. Destructive actions ask twice, and every crossing is written to a hash-chained receipts log. It is in development and is not yet available.
