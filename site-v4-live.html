The site, whole

November 12, 2026 — by Alexander Sanchez

The rebuilt site went live today — not page by page, but whole. Every page in the house style: classic white, the seal’s red, a serif face, and the MathSphere Nexus seal carried on every post as the signature mark.

The shape is the one described in yesterday’s entry: plain files on GitHub Pages, updated the way scripts are updated — written, checked, committed, pushed. No page-builder, no dashboard, nothing between the record and the page.

Two of the pages that went live today are the ones you are reading in spirit: the Diary, which carries the progress entry by entry, and the Blog, which does not update casually. The Prototypes page opened as an empty archive with a rule at its head — a prototype must pass house, or it doesn’t count as evidence. It did not stay empty long; that is tomorrow’s entry.

Still open, and said plainly: the email list is not yet wired in — the service choice is still to be made — and the house domain is not yet purchased. The site stands on the GitHub address until both are settled.

A command is what you type. The name is what the project is.

— Alexander
