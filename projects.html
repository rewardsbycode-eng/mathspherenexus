Wizard Central Pro AI Modes Toolkit

Status: In development — not yet available, no dates promised

A gate between a person and their AI models. Every tool call a model makes crosses one checkpoint; the person grants access, reviews what the model proposes, and approves anything that matters. Destructive actions ask twice, and every crossing is written to a hash-chained receipts log. 70 AI modes advise; guard modes bind.

The Wizards of Wizard Central

Status: In development — 39 of 41 built

The actors of the system: small, specialist tools, each a wizard that guides a person through one kind of work, step by step, instead of demanding commands they must already know. A command is what you type; the wizard is who walks with you.

MathSphere Nexus Arbiter

Status: In development

An integrity ledger for a person's own work: versioned records with pins and locks, content-addressed storage, whole-tree snapshots and restore, and a hash-chained receipt for every act — all local, nothing automatic by default, and anything that changes a record asks twice. MathSphere Nexus is the company; Arbiter is the tool.
